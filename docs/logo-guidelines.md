# GreyRocks logo guidelines

## Concept

GreyRocks uses a minimal isometric cube mark based on the supplied reference image. The shape suggests structure, stability, systems thinking and engineering without relying on a literal mountain, server, robot or AI brain.

## Research-informed principles

- Use the cube mark icon-only in small spaces and the horizontal cube + GREYROCKS lockup in the navbar/footer.
- Keep clear space around the mark; do not crop, rotate, skew or add effects.
- Prefer a single, recognizable silhouette over decorative detail.
- Keep the palette limited to the approved GreyRocks colors.
- Preserve adequate contrast against the surrounding surface.
- Use the dark mark on the light site surfaces; retain the separate asset variant only for future contexts where another surface is introduced.

These principles are consistent with the UI/UX Pro Max brand guidance on icon-only/full-logo variants, clear space, minimum size, approved color usage, and logo don'ts.

## Files

- `/public/brand/greyrocks-mark-dark.svg` — primary mark
- `/public/brand/greyrocks-mark.svg` — compatibility alias
- `/public/brand/greyrocks-wordmark-dark.svg` — primary wordmark
- `/public/brand/greyrocks-wordmark.svg` — compatibility alias
- `/public/brand/favicon.svg` — favicon

## Usage

Navbar minimum mark width: approximately 34px.

Wordmark should not be rendered smaller than approximately 108px wide on the web.

Do not add shadows, gradients, rotations or extra outlines to the logo.
